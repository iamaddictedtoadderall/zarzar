# Iron Ledger

A third-person mech action game in one HTML file, built with Three.js.

You are Wren, a contract pilot revived from cold storage by the Halvard–Mace Consortium for a three-part job on the ash world of Varn IV. Your handler stays on comms the whole way.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). You need a keyboard, a mouse and an internet connection, because Three.js and the fonts load from public CDNs. Click the view to capture the mouse.

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Move | W A S D |
| Aim | Mouse (arrow keys also work) |
| Jump, hold to hover | Space |
| Quick boost (dash) | Shift |
| Rifle | Left click |
| Pulse blade (lunges at your target) | Right click |
| Missile salvo | Q |
| Hard lock on current target | E or middle click |
| Repair kit | R |
| Pause | Esc or P |

## Tips

- Targets near the reticle lock on automatically, and the rifle leads them for you.
- Every hit builds a target's stagger gauge (the orange bar). A staggered target takes bonus damage, so that's the moment to use the blade.
- Hovering and quick boosting use EN. If EN runs out, your boosters lock up until it recharges.
- Quick boost sideways to shake off missiles.
- Each contract docks ammunition, repair kits and frame damage from your pay. Your handler keeps the books.

Progress (current contract, endings found) and settings are saved in your browser's local storage.
